Privacy Policy

Salary & Attendance Tracker · Last updated: 25 November 2026

What this app is

Salary & Attendance Tracker is a free web app that helps you record daily attendance, overtime, advances and estimate your salary for a salary cycle. App: anup143k.github.io/salary-tracker

Data we handle

The data you enter (your name, pay rates, attendance, overtime, advances and settings) is stored in your own browser or device. We do not run any server, and we cannot see, collect or store your data.

Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose "Google Drive backup", the app asks for one permission: drive.appdata (access to the app's own hidden folder in your Google Drive). It is used only to save and restore one backup file of your tracker data. The app cannot see or change any other file in your Drive. The backup goes directly between your device and your own Google Drive. To make signing in again faster, the email address of the Google account you connect is kept on your own device only. It is never sent to us or to any third party.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads provided by Adsterra (adsterra.com). Ad code from the ad provider runs on the app page in your browser. The ad provider may collect device or browser identifiers and use cookies or similar technologies, under its own privacy policy. Your tracker data is stored on your device and we do not send it to advertisers. Because ad code runs on the same page, we recommend you do not enter sensitive information in the app that you would not want an ad provider's code to be able to see.

Ads are switched off when you connect Google Drive backup, and Google user data is never used for advertising. An optional "Support the app" page on a separate website also shows ads.

What we do not do

We do not sell your tracker data or your Google Drive data, and we do not use them for advertising or analytics.

Your control and deleting data

You can disconnect Google Drive at any time from the app menu. To delete the Drive backup, remove the app's data in your Google Account under Security → Third-party access. To delete data on your device, clear this site's data in your browser or uninstall the app.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated. Questions: open an issue at github.com/Anup143K/salary-tracker/issues.
