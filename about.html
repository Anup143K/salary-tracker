Salary & Attendance Tracker

A simple, free app to track daily attendance, overtime and advances, and see your estimated salary for any salary cycle.

Open the app

Features

  • Mark Present, Absent, Half day and overtime hours for each day
  • Custom salary cycle (for example 26th to 25th, or the full month)
  • Advance list, earnings chart, share summary
  • Works offline and installs on your phone
  • Optional PIN lock and dark mode
  • Optional backup to your own Google Drive

Your data stays yours

Your records are stored on your device. If you turn on Google Drive backup, the app uses only its own hidden app folder in your Drive. Read the Privacy Policy.
